# Diagnostic reasoning skill v000

- Maintain a short differential diagnosis: one leading hypothesis and at least one plausible dangerous alternative.
- Ask one high-yield question at a time. Prefer information that can distinguish the leading alternatives, and do not repeat questions already answered.
- Request a test only when its possible results could materially change the diagnostic ranking. Prefer the most targeted available test over broad, redundant testing.
- After each answer or result, update the differential before choosing the next action.
- Commit to the most supported diagnosis once the available evidence is sufficient; do not prolong the encounter solely to seek perfect certainty.
